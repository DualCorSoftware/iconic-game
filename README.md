## 🎨 Design language

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top"><strong>Bold silhouettes</strong><br><br>Each subject is reduced to a chunky, recognizable mass with deliberately low detail.</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top"><strong>Controlled irregularity</strong><br><br>Straight edges and sharp corners are offset by a subtle whole-form tilt and small vertex variation.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top"><strong>Transparent separation</strong><br><br>Crossing forms are separated by gaps of pure background instead of outlines or secondary colors.</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top"><strong>Consistent presence</strong><br><br>Every icon fills the canvas with a shared visual weight and remains legible at interface sizes.</td>
</tr>
</table>

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The artwork is filled geometry only. Icon files contain no strokes, transforms, groups, embedded styles, scripts, external resources, or editor metadata.

## 📐 Technical profile

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top"><strong>Canvas and live area</strong><br><br>Every icon uses a 128 × 128 canvas with an 8px margin on all sides, leaving a 112 × 112 live area.</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top"><strong>Scale and coverage</strong><br><br>The dominant dimension measures 100 to 112px. Ink covers 18% to 42% of the canvas, with approximately 28% as the typical target.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top"><strong>Feature weight</strong><br><br>Visible features are at least 8px thick. Bars typically measure 10 to 16px, with 6 to 9px of background separation.</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top"><strong>Tilt and vertex variation</strong><br><br>Whole forms tilt 1° to 3°, or 20° to 45° for long diagonal subjects. Vertex variation is ±1.3px, reduced to 0.7 to 1.0px on small rings.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top"><strong>Contained fills</strong><br><br>Substances remain approximately 6px inside the vessel wall so a visible ring of background separates the fill from its container.</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top"><strong>Crossings and round forms</strong><br><br>Cuts meet crossed edges at 20° or more. Round forms use low-frequency radius variation of up to ±4%.</td>
</tr>
</table>

## 🧩 Geometry rules

<details>
<summary><strong>Shape and negative space</strong></summary>

- Shapes use rectilinear geometry with straight edges and sharp corners.
- A single rotation applies to the whole icon so the subject leans as one unit.
- Shared vertices receive shared offsets so connected masses remain connected.
- Round forms use slight elliptical and low-frequency radial variation rather than per-vertex noise.
- Crossing forms receive a 6 to 9px transparent gap.
- Structural joins remain fused with no separating gap.
- Features that do not remain legible at 24px are omitted.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Crossings, joins, and interior details</strong></summary>

- A crossing passes over a form without belonging to it. The gap belongs to the lower shape.
- A structural component belongs to its parent form and remains fused to it.
- A cut meets the crossed edge at 20° or more.
- Closed rings and solid masses remain intact when a crossing would split them into unrelated pieces.
- Interior details remain at least 6px clear of their containing border.
- Fused masses overlap by approximately 9px and overrun into solid material only.
- Raised details are vertices of the parent outline rather than separate blocks.
- Every remaining segment after a cut retains enough length and width to read as part of its parent form.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>State families and contained fills</strong></summary>

- Related states share one byte-identical shell.
- A state's fill appends subpaths without changing the shell geometry.
- Discrete fill segments keep approximately 5px of separation.
- A substance inside a vessel is a separate mass with a flat top.
- Contained fills remain approximately 6px inside the vessel wall.
- The background ring between a substance and its vessel stays open.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>SVG output contract</strong></summary>

- Files live at `icons/svg/<name>.svg`.
- Each root uses `viewBox="0 0 128 128"`, `width="128"`, `height="128"`, and `fill="currentColor"`.
- Path geometry uses move, line, and close commands only. Curves and arcs are not used.
- Icons without overlaps use a single `<path>`.
- Icons with transparent separation use a `<mask>`, a masked base `<path>`, and an overlay `<path>`.
- Masks use `maskUnits="userSpaceOnUse"` with explicit bounds.
- Filled subpaths use consistent winding. Holes use reverse winding.
- Filenames are lowercase and use hyphens where needed.

</details>
